# Keyword brief: cat behavior assessment online

## Decision

Publish a dedicated guide at `/blog/cat-behavior-assessment-online`.

No existing page covers feline remote assessment as a complete intent. The general credential guide explains CCBC and DACVB qualifications but does not compare Fe-BARQ, AI observation, virtual environmental consultation and veterinary assessment. The dog assessment page is not an appropriate consolidation target because feline resource mapping, medical differentials and low-stress remote observation require their own treatment.

## Current result pattern

The current results include:

- Fe-BARQ and AAHA owner questionnaires
- feline-only behavior consultants offering worldwide virtual sessions
- 60-to-120-minute live assessments with intake, environment review and written plans
- packages with email follow-up
- veterinary cat-behavior consultations

More credible services request history in advance, review natural home video, assess resources and household relationships, provide a written plan, state qualifications and ask for veterinary clearance. Remote sessions can reduce the observer effect because an unfamiliar visitor does not enter the cat's territory.

## Target terms

- Primary: `cat behavior assessment online`

## Evidence and sources

- University of Pennsylvania Fe-BARQ: 100-item standardized owner proxy measure covering 23 behavioral factors plus miscellaneous behaviors.
- AAHA cat behavior client questionnaire: screening/conversation tool, not a complete history.
- AAFP/ISFM environmental needs guidelines: safe places, separated resources, predictable interactions and appropriate play.
- Clinical feline emotion-assessment literature: history, full-body observation, environment and medical context must be integrated.
- IAABC CCBC, CAAB/ACAAB and DACVB credential standards/directories.

## Feline-specific content requirements

- Medical-first thresholds for urination, appetite, pain, overgrooming and sudden aggression.
- Resource and route map for litter, food, water, rest, scratching, vertical space and hiding.
- Multi-cat assessment includes every cat, not only the visibly aggressive one.
- Do not provoke conflict or pull a cat from hiding for video.
- Explain that a questionnaire measures owner report, an AI report organizes visible markers, a consultant formulates a case, and a veterinarian assesses disease/prescribes.
