## Verified outline and gaps

1. TL;DR: a swat changes meaning with target, trigger, body tension, claws, and what happens next.
2. A quick context table: person, other cat, toy/object, and surprise approach.
3. Play and predatory investigation.
4. A request for space during touch or handling.
5. Fear, pain, and redirected arousal.
6. Intercat boundary-setting versus a fight.
7. What claws do and do not prove.
8. A safe response sequence and what not to do.
9. Veterinary, behavior, and human-wound thresholds.
10. What a photo can show and why video is usually better.

The page should improve on the current result set by treating a swat as an event in a sequence, avoiding a rigid emotion dictionary, separating people-directed and cat-directed cases, correcting dominance language, and giving an actionable observation log.
