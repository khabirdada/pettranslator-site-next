# Keyword brief: pet behavior journal app

## Decision

Publish a product-led educational guide at `/blog/pet-behavior-journal-app`.

The intent is distinct from the new vet-report page. A journal is the repeated capture system; a report is a concise output for a specific appointment. PetTranslator's pricing pages already describe pet profiles, analysis history, behavioral trends and a journal, so the page can explain the method while accurately positioning the product.

## Duplication audit

- `/blog/pet-behavior-report-for-vet` owns appointment preparation and report structure.
- `/blog/pet-behavior-photo-analysis` owns media selection.
- Stress and condition-specific pages mention tracking but do not explain journal design.
- No existing page targets app selection, consistent logging or trend interpretation.

## Current result pattern

Results include broad pet-health diaries, local-first observation logs, AI-assisted journals, medication/reminder apps and wearable dashboards. Common features are separate pet profiles, timestamped notes, photos/video, custom categories, routine and symptom fields, charts, exports and sharing. Most pages do not explain how to avoid subjective mood scoring, inconsistent definitions or selective logging.

## Search intent and terms

- Primary: `pet behavior journal app`
- Secondary: `pet behavior diary app`, `dog behavior tracker app`, `cat behavior journal`, `track pet behavior changes`
- Intent: commercial investigation plus practical setup.

## Evidence

- Merck Veterinary Manual: repeated questionnaires can identify patterns; useful history includes onset, frequency, duration, intensity, context, interventions and schedule.
- AAHA: consistent questionnaires over time make behavior changes easier to recognize; frequency, duration and context are useful.
- AAHA pain guidance: standardized, pet-specific questions capture trends better, while owner measures remain subject to bias.
- AAHA life-stage guidance: behavior, elimination, appetite, hydration, grooming, mobility, sleep and social changes can be medically relevant.

## Product boundary

PetTranslator offers pet profiles, stored analyses and behavioral trends/journal on paid plans. The journal organizes observations; AI interpretations remain uncertain and cannot diagnose disease, measure pain, predict aggression or replace veterinary assessment. Avoid quoting history-retention duration because two current pricing sections conflict; route readers to `/pricing` for current terms.
