## Gaps the PetTranslator article should fill

- Most results present a fixed dictionary. The article must explain that the same movement can have several functions.
- Many pages repeat Rugaas's observations without explaining the limits of the research. Include the 2017 pilot study and the 2023 human-approach study, with their limitations.
- Separate a signal's observable form from its inferred function.
- Give readers a repeatable context test: trigger, timing, accompanying signals, and change from the dog's baseline.
- Include a table that contrasts ordinary and pressure-related versions of each behavior.
- Explain what still photos miss, especially sequence, duration, sound, and recovery.
- Avoid unsupported rules such as requiring an exact number of signals within a fixed number of seconds.
