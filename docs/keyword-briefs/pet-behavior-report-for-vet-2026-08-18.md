# Keyword brief: pet behavior report for vet

## Decision

Publish a dedicated practical guide at `/blog/pet-behavior-report-for-vet`.

No existing article owns this intent. Several posts tell readers to share logs or media with a veterinarian, while the product and pricing pages mention a PDF export. The new page should connect those ideas without representing an owner-created or AI-generated document as a veterinary medical record.

## Duplication audit

- `/blog/pet-behavior-photo-analysis` covers choosing and interpreting still images.
- `/blog/ai-dog-body-language-analyzer` and `/blog/ai-cat-body-language-analyzer` explain automated observation.
- `/blog/online-dog-behaviorist-guide` and `/blog/cat-behavior-assessment-online` compare professional assessment routes.
- No page provides a concise report structure for a primary-care vet visit.

## Current result pattern

Search results are fragmented. Veterinary manuals and university intake forms dominate the useful evidence; commercial pet-log pages address adjacent health tracking. Strong sources consistently request onset, frequency, duration, intensity, context, daily schedule, environment, previous interventions, medication and medical history. They distinguish objective behavior descriptions from assumed motivation and warn against provoking behavior to record it.

## Target terms

- Primary: `pet behavior report for vet`
- Secondary: `pet behavior log for veterinarian`, `dog behavior report for vet`, `cat behavior report for vet`, `what to record for vet behavior appointment`

## Evidence and sources

- Merck Veterinary Manual: medical causes must be excluded; behavior history uses antecedent-behavior-consequence plus onset, frequency, duration, intensity, pattern change, interventions, schedule and environment.
- AAHA behavior management guidelines: standardized behavioral histories support comparison over time and should inform veterinary assessment.
- Cornell and NC State veterinary behavior history forms: presenting complaint, chronology, body language, aftermath, household, medical history and goals.
- Tufts: complete intake and medical records should reach the behavior clinic before the appointment.
- Virginia Tech: natural video may be recorded only when safe and without approaching or escalating the animal.

## Product boundary

PetTranslator.ai can organize visible markers, context, interpretation, confidence and Do/Avoid guidance. Pro includes a PDF export. Describe it as supporting material for a veterinary conversation, never a medical record, diagnosis, pain assessment, risk assessment or replacement for the clinic's own history and examination.
