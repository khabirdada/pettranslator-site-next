# Keyword brief: dog threshold training meaning

## Search intent

Informational with practical training intent. The reader has heard “under threshold” or “over threshold” in reactivity, fear or socialization advice and wants a usable definition, visible signs and a way to choose training distance.

## Duplication audit

`/blog/leash-reactivity-in-dogs` has a short threshold section tied to dogs on leash. `/blog/dog-trigger-stacking` defines threshold in one FAQ. Separation, guarding and puppy pages use the term within specific protocols. No page explains that threshold depends on trigger intensity, distance, duration, movement, environment and accumulated state, or separates emotional threshold from door-threshold training.

Create a general concept guide. Link back to condition-specific protocols. Patch the leash-reactivity page's claims that food refusal definitively proves threshold and that the prefrontal cortex goes offline, replacing them with observable flexibility and cautious language.

## Current result-page patterns

Current results from AKC, humane organizations and trainer sites divide behavior into under, at and over threshold. Most focus on distance and food response. Peer-reviewed veterinary fear and noise-fear sources support graduated exposure at an intensity that does not elicit the target fear response, but threshold is not one directly measurable neurological switch.

## Content opportunity

- Define threshold operationally as the point where flexible behavior narrows in a specific setup.
- Explain that it is a gradient and working estimate, not a fixed line inside the dog.
- Use response latency, body tension, fixation, movement and recovery together.
- Explain why eating is useful but not decisive.
- Show the “four Ds”: distance, duration, distraction/intensity and difficulty, plus movement and trigger stacking.
- Give a repeatable starting-distance and session-log method.
- Separate exposure from flooding and training from emergency exit.
- Cover shutdown as well as barking/lunging.
- Flag pain and medical change when threshold suddenly shrinks.
