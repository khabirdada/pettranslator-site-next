# Keyword brief: AI dog body language analyzer

## Decision

Publish a dedicated product-intent guide at `/blog/ai-dog-body-language-analyzer` and link directly to `https://app.pettranslator.ai/analyze`.

Do not replace or duplicate `/blog/dog-body-language`, which teaches canine signals, or `/blog/do-pet-translator-apps-work`, which evaluates the wider translator category. This page answers how an AI dog body-language analyzer works, how to provide usable input, what a PetTranslator report contains, and where the method stops.

## Intent and result pattern

The current results are predominantly app and product pages. Common promises include photo or video upload, emotion or stress labels, follow-up advice, history, and downloadable reports. Several make unverified accuracy, mind-reading or health-screening claims. The opportunity is a more credible conversion page built around observable markers, context, uncertainty and referral boundaries.

## Evidence notes

- Research shows humans often attend disproportionately to dog faces even though longer inspection of the body is associated with better emotion categorization.
- Emotion inference depends on full-body, dynamic and contextual information; trained observers can still disagree.
- Dogs integrate information across sensory channels, supporting the value of video and context over isolated facial labels.
- AVSAB recommends reward-based training and rejects aversive methods.

## Product facts verified in the repository

- Dogs and cats supported.
- Photo or short-video upload in a modern browser.
- Visible markers listed before interpretation.
- User context is weighed alongside physical signals.
- Confidence and Do/Avoid action plan included.
- Three free analyses to start; no card.
- Premium PDF export.
- Uploads are not used to train AI models.
- Not a veterinary diagnostic or replacement for a behavior professional.
