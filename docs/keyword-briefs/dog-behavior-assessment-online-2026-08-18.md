# Keyword brief: dog behavior assessment online

## Decision

Consolidate into `/blog/online-dog-behaviorist-guide` instead of publishing a competing URL.

The existing page already covers remote consultations, credentials, costs, intake, telemedicine constraints, safety, red flags and professional directories in depth. It lacked a clear explanation of the different products described as an "online assessment": questionnaire, automated media observation, live non-veterinary behavior consultation, and veterinary behavior assessment.

## Current result pattern

The current top results mix four intents:

- validated or generic owner questionnaires
- service pages for 60-to-90-minute live virtual consultations
- packages combining intake, consultation, written plan and follow-ups
- canine behavior or temperament evaluations with unclear clinical scope

Commercial pages commonly promise root-cause identification and individualized plans. More credible pages name practitioner credentials, collect history and video before the session, state that the service is not a guaranteed outcome, provide a written plan and involve a veterinarian when appropriate.

## Authoritative sources

- University of Pennsylvania C-BARQ: validated owner-completed baseline and follow-up behavioral evaluation.
- AAHA dog behavior client questionnaire: screening tool intended to direct clinical conversation, explicitly not a substitute for complete history.
- AVSAB humane dog training position statement.
- ACVB, IAABC, CCPDT and Animal Behavior Society credential standards/directories.
- Existing article sources on VCPR, remote consultation, reward-based training and behavior medicine.
