# Keyword brief: AI cat body language analyzer

## Decision

Publish a dedicated product-intent guide at `/blog/ai-cat-body-language-analyzer`.

The existing `/blog/cat-body-language` teaches the signal vocabulary. `/blog/do-pet-translator-apps-work` evaluates translator products broadly. The new page should help a cat owner use a photo/video analyzer, understand its output and limitations, and start an analysis without repeating the field guide.

## Current result pattern

The current results are mainly app listings and commercial tools. They emphasize single-photo mood labels, ears, eyes, tail, posture and advice. Some promise exact thoughts, broad health screening, pain detection or unexplained accuracy figures. A credible page can compete by showing observations, feline-specific context, uncertainty and explicit medical limits.

## Target terms

- Primary: `AI cat body language analyzer`
- Secondary: `cat body language analyzer`, `analyze cat body language from photo`, `cat emotion analyzer`, `AI cat behavior analysis`, `cat mood analyzer`

## Feline-specific jobs

- Explain why cats require whole-body reading including whiskers, ear rotation, head position, body compression and tail.
- Show why pupil size is ambiguous without lighting and context.
- Prefer sequences for tail movement, approach/retreat, freezing and recovery.
- Explain coat, breed, face shape, cropped framing and hiding limitations.
- Separate behavioral observation from pain assessment and veterinary diagnosis.
- Provide capture guidance that does not force a cat out of hiding or recreate stress.

## Evidence notes

- A recent large study found human recognition of relaxed, tense and fearful feline states above chance but still relatively low, supporting explicit marker display and uncertainty.
- Clinical feline guidance treats history, body language and behavior together and notes that the research base has historically been limited.
- Full-body posture, face, ears and tail contribute to feline affective displays.
- AAFP/ISFM environmental guidance supports choice, safe places and predictable human interaction.

## Product claims verified locally

- Dogs and cats supported through photo and short-video uploads.
- Browser-based; three free analyses to start and no card.
- Visible observations, interpretation, confidence and Do/Avoid output.
- Premium PDF export.
- Uploads are not used to train AI models.
- Not a medical diagnostic or substitute for a veterinarian/behavior professional.
